## The mission

**Cue exists to help women with ADHD stop white-knuckling their lives — by giving them tools grounded in behavioral science and ACT, environments that fit how their brains actually work, and a community of women who get it.**

## The premise, in one quote

> _"Every time you rely on your memory to hold something in your brain, you're spending executive function that you simply cannot spare."_
> — Sasha Hamdani, MD, ADHD psychiatrist

This is the scientific thesis of Cue in one line. ADHD isn't a willpower problem, it's an executive-function economy operating under scarcity. Every cognitive load we ask an ADHD brain to carry is a withdrawal from an account already overdrawn.

**The ACT layer adds the values compass**: what do we _want_ to spend our spared executive function on? Not everything is worth doing, and Cue helps women get clear on which "everythings" are.

Every product decision has to pass one test: **does it spend a user's executive function, or save it — in service of what she says matters most?**

## The opportunity, in one paragraph

An estimated 1 in 4 adults now suspect they have ADHD; ~6% actually meet criteria. Women are diagnosed on average five years later than men, present inattentive rather than hyperactive, and carry years of shame, anxiety, and internalized failure before they get a name for what's happening. Existing tools fail them: Inflow (now inside Cerebral's medicalized funnel) delivers sequenced CBT modules that ADHD brains struggle to complete; Shimmer's coaching costs $140–345/month; therapy is waitlisted and rarely ADHD-specialized for women; TikTok content is 50–89% misleading. Meanwhile, the science has moved: ACT and executive-function scaffolding are more clinically appropriate for ADHD than the CBT-flavored approaches that dominate the market, and the female-specific dimensions of ADHD (cycle-linked symptom variability, rejection-sensitive dysphoria, late-diagnosis identity work, life-stage transitions) remain almost entirely absent from consumer products. That's the gap.

## Who we serve

**Primary customer**: Adult women, 25–45, with ADHD — diagnosed, self-diagnosed, on an assessment waitlist, or beginning to suspect. Cognitively high-functioning ("but why is my life this hard?" profile). Long history of masking. Comorbid anxiety likely. Cycle-related symptom variability likely. Late-diagnosis identity work is the emotional throughline.

**Included by design**: AuDHD women. Roughly 40% of autistic adults also meet ADHD criteria; 71% of AuDHD adults are women. Their sensory and processing needs are met through: sensory-safe design defaults, low-stim mode, neurodiversity-affirming language, no ABA methods, community moderation that welcomes both ADHD and AuDHD voices. We don't claim to specialize in autism; we build in a way that doesn't exclude AuDHD women.

**Explicit non-audience** (v1): autistic women without ADHD, children/adolescents, men, insurance-reimbursed community mental health.

**Where they are today**: r/ADHDwomen, Kaleidoscope Society, ADHD TikTok, Inflow (dissatisfied), on a therapy or assessment waitlist, or nowhere.

**Pilot geography**: Oregon (Portland-dense community, strong OON norms, tight professional network). Expand to Washington in year 2.

## The ACT + executive-function positioning

This is the strategic core.

Every meaningful competitor leads with a "change the person" premise:

- **Cognitive therapy** (CBT, Inflow): change your thoughts to change your behavior
- **Coaching** (Shimmer, ADHD coaches): try harder with a human accountability partner
- **ABA** (dominant in autism care, actively rejected by neurodiversity community): change your behavior to appear normal
- **Self-help ADHD content**: fix your habits, fix yourself

**Cue's frame is different**: your brain is not the problem. Executive function is a limited resource; environments and defaults determine whether it gets spent well. ACT gives us the values layer — what do you actually want your life to be about? — and executive-function scaffolding gives us the environment layer — how do we design your context so that values-aligned action doesn't require willpower you don't have?

The two-part statement, spoken plainly:

> **We don't ask you to think differently. We help you notice what your brain does, accept it, and design your life around what actually matters to you.**

Why ACT specifically:

- **Third-wave behavioral therapy** — the current frontier of behavioral science, not a legacy modality
- **Values-based, not symptom-based** — a woman comes to Cue asking "how do I live according to what matters?" not "how do I fix myself?"
- **Acceptance + committed action** — accept what your brain does; commit to values-aligned behavior
- **Anti-pathology by design** — you are not broken, you are wired differently
- **Neurodiversity-compatible** — no push toward neurotypical performance, no masking encouraged
- **Strong evidence base in adult ADHD** — including cycle-linked symptom variability, RSD, life transitions
- **Michelle's actual clinical training** — this positioning is authentic, not marketing

## What we're building

Three surfaces, layered:

### 1. Community (free tier, the front door)

- Curated, moderated, women-first spaces for ADHD women
- Sensory-safe by default (low-stim mode, no autoplay, granular notifications, predictable layouts)
- Multiple sub-spaces so ADHD, AuDHD, late-diagnosis, and life-stage experiences can coexist
- Body-doubling sessions (drop-in, multiple per day)
- Weekly rituals, guest sessions with clinicians, researchers, lived-experience voices
- Distinct from Reddit: structured, safe, no algorithmic doomscroll

### 2. Behavioral & values tools (paid tier)

- Short, ACT-grounded values clarification (what matters, in your words)
- Small committed-action experiments (not sequenced text modules)
- Environmental design tools: externalize memory, reduce decision load, build defaults
- If-then implementation plans (Gollwitzer, evidence-based for ADHD)
- Defusion techniques for RSD and shame spirals
- Body-doubling integrated into task initiation

### 3. The female-specific lens (paid tier, the moat)

- Cycle overlay on symptom, function, and values-aligned action tracking
- RSD support (ACT-based defusion, not cognitive reframing)
- Late-diagnosis identity work — grief, relief, unmasking process, "who would I have been"
- Life-stage awareness: postpartum, perimenopause, contraception changes
- Optional monthly pulse (validated scales, framed as self-knowledge)

## What we refuse to do (the "not doing" list — pinned)

1. We do not diagnose or imply diagnosis.
2. We do not use clickbait or "you might have…" content marketing.
3. We do not sell user data, ever.
4. We do not build AI therapist, AI companion, or AI-generated interpretations of user data.
5. We do not build for men in v1.
6. We do not use ABA methods, endorse them, or partner with ABA providers.
7. We do not claim autism specialization we don't have — we design inclusively for AuDHD without over-claiming.
8. We do not launch without moderation and sensory-accessibility infrastructure.
9. We do not gate the community behind a paywall.
10. We do not partner with medication or telepsychiatry brands in v1.
11. We do not use pathology-first language ("symptoms," "manage," "high-functioning," "suffers from").

Every one of these is a business someone else can build. Only one is ours.

## Business model

**Freemium, consumer-paid:**

- **Free**: community, body-doubling, foundational values-and-environment content, weekly rituals
- **Premium**: personalized ACT-based practices, environmental experiments, cycle/RSD/life-stage tools, longitudinal self-tracking, ~$12–15/month or ~$99/year
- **Wave 2**: async peer/coach add-on, curated ADHD-affirming clinician referral marketplace (curated, not commission-driven), CE-eligible clinician training modules (Michelle's ACT-for-ADHD-women curriculum)

**No medication sales, no clickbait, no data resale, no coach-marketplace commissions.**

**Rough unit economics**: at $12/mo premium and ~15% free-to-paid conversion, ~15,000 free users → ~2,250 paid users ≈ $27K MRR ≈ $324K ARR (year-2 target).
