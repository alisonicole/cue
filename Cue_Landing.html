Our approach

We don't change you. We change the room.

Most support for ADHD and autism starts with the same premise: something about you needs to be fixed. Cue starts somewhere else — with the environment around you.

01

Environment-first, not willpower-first

Instead of asking your brain to work harder, we design the environments around you to work with it — through simple behavioral tools, scaffolds, and rituals grounded in real science.

02

Neurodiversity-affirming

No masking. No fixing. No "high-functioning." Your brain isn't a problem to be solved — it's a wiring to be understood, honored, and supported.

03

Built for women, by women

Cycle-aware. Attuned to rejection sensitivity. Made for late diagnosis, masking recovery, and every hormonal shift the research keeps pretending doesn't matter.

Our story

We built Cue because we needed it, and it didn't exist yet.

We're two women — a therapist who kept meeting the same story in her clients, and a strategist who lived it. Between us, we saw what the science already knew, what women were quietly figuring out on their own, and how completely the existing tools missed both.

So we're building the thing we wanted. Quiet, warm, structured, honest. Grounded in behavioral science, held by community, made for women whose brains have always worked a little differently.
