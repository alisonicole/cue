# Cue — 90-Day Co-Creation Sprint Plan

_A working plan for Michelle & Alison_
_Draft, subject to revision after our kickoff working session_

---

## What this sprint is

Ninety days of working together on Cue as if we're partners — while both keeping our day jobs and neither of us formally signing anything until day 90.

**The point**: to find out, through the work itself, whether we're actually partners. Not by talking about it more, but by shipping alongside each other.

**The commitment during the sprint**: 10–15 hrs/week each. Weekly working session. Small cash contributions (~$3–5K each total) to cover legal setup, domain hosting, design tools, and initial infra. No salaries. No entity yet.

**The decision at day 90**: formalize as co-founders (real paperwork, entity formation, equity vesting) — or walk away clean, with the friendship intact and the work we've done as reference material either of us can use going forward.

---

## Roles during the sprint

Same as our proposed long-term split, without the formal titles:

- **Michelle leads**: ACT + behavioral curriculum outline, community principles + moderation posture, brand voice on clinical content, language discipline, user-research conversations with her clinical/research network.
- **Alison leads**: landing page + waitlist infrastructure, brand and visual identity, technical scoping (community platform selection, MVP tech stack), user-research conversations with AGMH-adjacent audience, legal and financial setup.
- **We do together**: the "not doing" list, weekly working sessions, all major scope decisions, mission/positioning language, the day-90 decision itself.

---

## Weekly cadence

- **One 90-minute working session per week** (video, recorded if we both agree, notes shared to repo)
- **One async check-in per week** (Slack / text / whatever's lowest-friction)
- **Shared repo for artifacts**: github.com/alisonicole/cue (Alison adds Michelle as collaborator week 1)
- **Decision log**: every meaningful decision gets a one-liner in the repo (`decisions.md`) so we have a paper trail
- **No Slack / no daily channel** — protects both our day jobs and both our brains

## Month 1 (Days 1–30) — Ground

**Theme**: get aligned on the deepest choices, before we build anything user-facing.

### What we ship together this month

- Signed mutual-understanding letter (simple 1-page MOU covering scope, IP, walk-away terms) — this replaces a formal contract during the sprint
- Locked v4 strategy brief (any last revisions from Michelle folded in)
- Locked "not doing" list (both signed off)
- ADHD-first + AuDHD-inclusive scope written up as its own short doc, with the language rules
- Domain audit and consolidation (which of `cueyourhealth.com`, `cueforyou.com`, and any adjacent domains we point at what)
- LLC formation research (we don't file yet — but we know what we're forming and where when we do)

### What Michelle drafts

- **ACT-in-Cue outline** — a 3–5 page working doc on how ACT principles map to product features (values clarification, defusion, committed action, present-moment awareness). Not the curriculum itself yet — the philosophy of what the curriculum will do.
- **Community principles v1** — 1 page. What makes an ADHD-women community healthy? What breaks it? What's our stance on moderation?

### What Alison drafts

- **Landing page v2** (already 80% built — just needs Michelle's voice check)
- **Waitlist infrastructure** — Mailchimp or ConvertKit account, real form on landing page, welcome sequence
- **Brand identity draft** — logo, palette, type system, tone document (leverage existing design work)
- **Technical scoping doc** — one-page assessment of community platform options (Circle, Discourse, custom Supabase) with pros/cons for our sensory-safety requirements

### End-of-month checkpoint (Day 30)

30-minute retro. One question: **are we finding each other or are we drifting?** Honest answers only. If drift, name it now.

---

## Month 2 (Days 31–60) — Listen

**Theme**: 20 conversations with the women we're building for. Don't build without listening.

### What we ship together this month

- **20 user-research conversations completed** — 10 recruited by Michelle from her clinical/research network, 10 recruited by Alison from AGMH-adjacent audience. Structured protocol (30 min each). Shared notes doc.
- **User-research synthesis** — pain patterns, voice patterns, what's already working for them, what would make Cue actually useful. This becomes the input for MVP scope.
- **Landing page live in soft launch** — quiet, no marketing push. We share the URL with 20–50 people we trust. First 100 waitlist signups.

### What Michelle drafts

- **Behavioral curriculum v1** — first 12–16 weeks of Cue content structured around ACT and executive-function scaffolding. This is her deliverable and will take real time — probably the biggest single artifact of the sprint.
- **Content voice examples** — 5–10 short pieces (200–400 words each) in Cue voice on topics like RSD, values clarification, task initiation. Both to build the content library and to validate the brand voice.

### What Alison drafts

- **Community platform decision** — locked, based on scoping doc + user research
- **MVP scope document** — what ships in v0 (probably: waitlist + curated community + 4–6 behavioral tools + weekly ritual)
- **Fractional engineering conversation** — 2–3 conversations with contract engineers to understand build cost + timeline

### End-of-month checkpoint (Day 60)

60-minute working session. Review user research together. Sharpen MVP scope based on what we heard. **Decision point**: does what we heard confirm the strategy, or does it push a specific pivot? Name it now, not after we build.

---

## Month 3 (Days 61–90) — Decide

**Theme**: bring it to the surface. Ready to say yes or ready to walk away.

### What we ship together this month

- **MVP technical scope frozen** — what we're building, in what order, at what cost
- **Cost estimate for months 4–9** (build phase) — what we'd need to actually get to soft launch. Both time and money.
- **Fundraising posture decision** — are we raising a pre-seed after MVP hits engagement metrics, or bootstrapping through year 1?
- **Legal / entity paperwork drafted** (not filed) — LLC operating agreement, IP assignment, founder agreements, vesting schedule. Ready to file day 91 if we're going.
- **The Day 90 conversation** — honest, structured, no drift. Prepared with our own written yes/no rationales in advance.

### What Michelle drafts

- **Curriculum v1 completion** — the 12–16 week outline is a real, usable artifact
- **Community rules and moderation playbook** — how we handle conflict, sensory-safety, ABA topics, medication discussions

### What Alison drafts

- **Financial model** for year 1 — what we spend on what, when, and what runway we have
- **Founder agreement** — first draft based on our sprint decisions

### The Day 90 decision framework

Before Day 90, each of us writes independently — no consultation — a one-page **"my honest state"** document covering:

1. What I loved about working together
2. What I found hard
3. Whether I want to formalize as co-founders and why / why not
4. What conditions I'd need to say yes (if any)
5. What I'd want if we walked away — what's ours vs. shared vs. either of ours to take

We swap those documents 24 hours before the Day 90 meeting. Meeting is 2 hours. No decisions made in the room — we walk away with 48 hours to sit before finalizing.

**Three possible outcomes at Day 90:**

- **Go**: file the LLC, execute founder agreements, wire the first infrastructure cash, start month 4 build phase.
- **Extend**: another 30–60 days of working together, with clarified scope on what we're testing.
- **Walk clean**: mutual thank-you, honest reason, shared artifacts, no bad blood. Each of us keeps the friendship and can reference the work as we go on to build (or not build) other things.

## Money during the sprint

**Total sprint spend estimate: $3,000–5,000 each, ~$6,000–10,000 total**

- Domain consolidation / renewals: ~$100
- Landing page hosting (Vercel or similar): $0–20/mo
- Mailchimp or ConvertKit (waitlist): $0–30/mo
- Design tools (Figma): $15–30/mo
- Community platform pilot (Circle free tier or Discourse hosting): $0–100/mo
- One-time legal consult on the "not diagnosing" line: $1,000–3,000
- One-time LLC/founder-agreement legal drafting (deferred billing until Day 90 if we go): $2,000–5,000
- User-research incentives (Amazon gift cards for 20 conversations): $500 total

We agree in advance: any single expense over $200 needs both of us to sign off. Below $200, either of us can spend and get reimbursed at end of month.

## What we're deliberately NOT doing during the sprint

- Not filing an LLC (waiting until Day 90 decision)
- Not launching publicly (soft launch to trusted circle only)
- Not building the MVP itself (scoping and preparation only)
- Not hiring anyone
- Not talking to investors
- Not paying ourselves anything
- Not committing to leave our day jobs
- Not signing anything binding beyond the 1-page MOU

Every one of these is a Month 4+ decision, conditional on Day 90 go.

---

## The one-page MOU (draft, we'll finalize together)

_By Day 7 of the sprint, both of us sign a 1-page mutual understanding covering:_

- **Purpose**: 90-day co-creation sprint to determine partnership fit
- **Scope**: everything above
- **IP**: all work product during the sprint is jointly owned. If we go: assigned to the new entity. If we walk: shared, either of us can use it, neither of us can claim exclusive ownership.
- **Confidentiality**: mutual — nothing shared publicly, nothing shared with third parties without both agreeing
- **Money**: how expenses are split, what happens to unused funds if we walk
- **Walk-away terms**: what happens to the domains, the repo, the strategy brief, the landing page if either of us decides to end the sprint
- **Handshake, not lawyer contract**: this is a memorandum of trust, not a legal instrument. If we're going to need lawyers to interpret each other's intent, we're not partners.

## What excites me about this sprint

We each get to see the other person work — under real time constraints, real ambiguity, real disagreement — before we sign anything permanent. And we get to build something real regardless of the outcome.

If we're partners, we'll know by Day 90 in a way we couldn't know by Day 1.
If we're not, we'll have built something meaningful together and both keep our friendship and our ideas.

Either way, we win.
